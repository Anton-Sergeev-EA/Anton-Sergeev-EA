### :man_technologist: About Me

<p><b>Power Systems Engineer turned Industrial AI / ML Engineer</b></p>
<p><i>20+ years designing and operating mission-critical automation for electric power infrastructure (0.4–750 kV), now building the AI layer on top of it.</i></p>

<p>Most ML engineers have never stood in front of a substation control panel. Most SCADA engineers have never trained a model. I've done both — that's the gap I fill.</p>

<div><b>💻 Core Competencies</b></div>
    <p>⚡ <b>SCADA & APCS:</b> Real-time monitoring, Modbus TCP/RTU, control systems, data acquisition</p>
    <p>⚡ <b>Industrial AI:</b> Predictive maintenance, anomaly detection, telemetry pipelines</p>
    <p>⚡ <b>ML & Data Science:</b> Time series forecasting, classification, ensemble models</p>
    <p>⚡ <b>RAG & LLMs:</b> Domain-specific AI assistants over local models (Ollama) and vector search</p>
    <p>⚡ <b>Native C++:</b> SIMD-accelerated vector indexes, custom signal-processing cores, pybind11 extensions</p>
    <p>⚡ <b>Backend & Systems:</b> Python, C++, FastAPI, async services, PostgreSQL</p>
    <p>⚡ <b>DevOps/MLOps:</b> Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, edge deployment (NVIDIA Jetson)</p>
